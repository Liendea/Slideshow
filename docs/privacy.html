Privacy Policy

Slideshow for iPad · Last updated: 8 October 2026

In short: Slideshow does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. The photos you choose are only used to show the slideshow on your own iPad and never leave the device through the app.

Who we are

Slideshow is published by [Publisher name]. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at [contact email].

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting. It does not send any information to us or to any third party.

Access to your photos and files

With your permission, the app reads photos from the Photos app and from files you choose in the Files app (including iCloud Drive). This access is used for one purpose only: displaying the photos you have selected in the slideshow on your iPad.

  • Photos app: the app saves a reference to the albums and photos you select so it can show them again next time. The photos themselves stay in your photo library.
  • Files app: photos you pick from Files are copied into the app's private storage on your iPad so the slideshow also works offline.
  • Settings: your slideshow settings (time per photo, transition, loop and shuffle) are saved on your iPad.

All of this is stored only on your iPad. The app never uploads, shares or transmits your photos, and we never have access to them.

iCloud

If your photos or files are stored in iCloud, Apple's system may download them to your iPad when they are shown or selected. This happens between your device and your own iCloud account and is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. Like other app data, the app's saved settings and copied photos may also be included in your own iPad backups (for example iCloud Backup) if you have backups turned on.

Removing your data

You can remove albums, photos and folders from the slideshow at any time with the remove buttons or "Clear" in the app. Copied photos are deleted from the app's storage when you remove them. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPad. You can change or revoke photo access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
